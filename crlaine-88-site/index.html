The CR Laine 88 Series blends clean, transitional styling with the flexibility of true custom upholstery. Designed as part of CR Laine's Custom Design Collection, the 88 Series offers a wide range of sofas, sectionals, loveseats, chairs and swivel chairs that can be tailored in hundreds of fabric and leather options, cushion styles and finishes to fit your space and lifestyle.

 

With its comfortable proportions, refined silhouette and American-made craftsmanship, the 88 Series makes it easy to create a personalized seating arrangement that feels both timeless and inviting.
