Lexington's Personal Design Series program allows you to create fully customized upholstery designs with almost limitless options. The program includes sofas, chairs, ottomans, and sectionals - each piece tailored by skilled artisans in North Carolina.

Select your frame size, arm style, base style, cushion configuration, pillow, and trim options to reflect your personal style and fit your specific room configuration. Choose from over 1,000 fabrics or your own material, and over 30 custom wood finishes. Finally, envision your custom design using our 3d visualization tool.
