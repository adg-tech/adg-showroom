Tori Petite

The Petite Tori Chair is a slightly smaller version of our best-selling Tori Chair. It features a tall back and clean lines that blend transitional styling with lots of comfort. Part of our Custom Source product, the Petite Tori can be customized by changing the arm, back and base options. It is also available as a swivel, swivel glider, swivel rocker and recliner. Matching ottoman is available.
